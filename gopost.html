1. Who we are and scope

Law365 develops GOPost, an Android application for managing connected social accounts. This policy covers GOPost and its supporting services. Availability depends on the release, platform permissions and connected account. A feature described here does not become available simply because it appears in this policy.

For privacy questions or requests, contact gopost72@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Sign-in: Google/Firebase authentication identifiers and verified email address; your Google display name is used for the GOPost profile. Authentication services may process additional sign-in, device and connection information under their own policies. GOPost does not receive your Google password.
  • Local workspace: draft text, selected platforms, selected media references and file metadata, scheduling choices, timezone and appearance preferences. Drafts and preferences are saved in encrypted app-private storage. Selecting media gives access to the files you choose, not unrestricted access to your entire library.
  • Connected social accounts: platform account identifiers and names, granted permissions, connection status and authorization tokens. Where supported and authorized, the online workspace handles post text and status, comments and supported messages (including author names and message text), and account performance metrics. A social account connection requires the relevant platform's authorization process.
  • Service records: trial and subscription entitlement state, feature usage counters, operation identifiers and outcomes, connection confirmations, and privacy/deletion requests. Minimal keyed hashes of social account identities help prevent repeated free trials through new GOPost accounts. These are pseudonymous identifiers, not anonymous data.
  • Technical and support information: service providers may process IP addresses, request times and security/connection logs to deliver and protect services. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments. Please do not send passwords, API keys or unnecessary sensitive information.

Feature availability depends on the development release, enabled backend configuration and platform permissions. YouTube-native upload and scheduling do not require GOPost video storage or a scheduling worker. The current development app can keep drafts on the device; it must not describe a locally saved draft as an uploaded or published post. Subscription checkout is not currently active.

3. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate you, display and maintain your workspace, carry out supported actions you request, manage connected accounts, show permitted platform data, enforce trial/access and usage limits, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and handle privacy requests. Optional AI processing is described separately below.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, necessary account and service processing supports the service you request; proportionate security and trial-abuse prevention support our legitimate interests; optional AI sharing uses the specific consent described below; and some records may be needed to meet legal obligations. Applicable rights and requirements depend on your location.

We do not sell personal information, provide it for targeted advertising, or use social account content to train our own AI models. The current app does not include advertising or a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

5. Optional AI sharing with Z.ai

YouTube AI requests (consent version zai-youtube-v3): when enabled, a request shares only the selected comment text for a reply draft, or the selected 7/30-day report's views, watch minutes, likes, comment count and subscribers gained for content experiments. Names, channel identifiers, video/audio files and OAuth credentials are not sent separately. Comment text may itself contain personal information. Review each request and cancel if you do not want it shared. AI drafts are optional, editable, and never sent to YouTube without your confirmation. YouTube-derived data is not used to train our models.

GOPost may relay video-upload chunks directly to YouTube without storing the video file. For YouTube-native schedules, the completed upload is held private by YouTube until the public publication time you approve. Disconnecting GOPost or closing it does not by itself cancel an accepted YouTube schedule; cancel it in GOPost while connected or in YouTube Studio.

AI is optional and requires request-specific consent. When enabled, every request requires a separate in-app disclosure and your agreement before the backend calls Z.ai. Canceling leaves that request unsent. Google sign-in or connecting a social account does not itself authorize AI sharing.

  • Reply drafts: only the selected public Instagram/Facebook comment text is sent with instructions to generate a draft. Author names, account names, account identifiers and access tokens are not added as separate fields. The comment itself may contain names or other personal information; share it only if you have the necessary rights and permissions.
  • Growth guidance: only numeric platform metrics displayed for the selected account and 7-day or 30-day period, together with their metric names, are sent with generation instructions. Depending on availability, these include Instagram post/reach/comment totals or configured Facebook Page insight totals. Additional metric sharing remains disabled until this disclosure is published. Audience identities, comment text and account credentials are not included.
  • Excluded: private messages are blocked from this AI integration. YouTube sharing is limited to the selected comment or numeric report metrics disclosed above. Media files, Google sign-in credentials and social authorization tokens are not included in AI requests.
  • Output: suggestions are shown for review. A generated reply is not sent automatically. Outputs can be inaccurate and do not guarantee growth. Successful output is stored in the backend operation record for request consistency and usage accounting and is returned to the app.

The configured model is GLM-4.7-Flash. No automatic paid-model fallback or automatic request retry is enabled. Provider availability and limits can affect whether a request succeeds.

Z.ai's Privacy Policy includes its Data Processing Addendum for API Services, which describes API customer-content handling, other customer records and international processing. Its API addendum states that customer input/output content is processed in real time rather than saved on its servers; other customer data may be retained to provide services or meet legal requirements. This is Z.ai's stated practice, not a guarantee by Law365 that no metadata is retained. See also its Terms of Service.

You can decline any later AI request. Withdrawing consent for future sharing does not undo a completed request; contact us about associated records or privacy rights.

6. Your choices, access and deletion

  • Social connections: use the connected-account controls when the online service is available. You can separately revoke Google/YouTube access through Google account connections, or use Instagram/Facebook settings to remove permissions. Disconnecting does not delete posts already on a social platform or reset an existing trial.
  • Local data: remove individual drafts in the app, clear GOPost storage in Android settings, or uninstall. Clearing local storage does not delete a Firebase identity or online workspace. Exports you save outside the app must be managed separately.
  • Export: the development app exports available local data; server export requires an active backend and includes supported workspace collections. It is not a promise that every internal security or provider record is included.
  • Account deletion: the in-app action records a request when the backend is available and clears local GOPost data. The lifecycle job processes that request by disabling sign-in, deleting the online workspace (including saved AI output and social credentials), removing associated connection and OAuth records, and deleting the Firebase login identity. A request acknowledgement is not confirmation that processing has finished. You can also use the account-deletion request page. While the backend/job is unavailable, email gopost72@gmail.com with the subject “GOPost account deletion” from your account email. We may request proportionate verification; never send a password or token. We will explain the request's status and any lawful exceptions.
  • Privacy rights: depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, object to certain processing or withdraw consent, and complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact the email below to exercise applicable rights.

Revoking platform access stops future authorized use once the revocation is effective; it does not automatically erase records already held by GOPost or a provider. Requests for GOPost-held platform data can be made through the same support email.

7. Retention

Local drafts remain until you remove them or clear app storage. GOPost storage copies of published media are cleaned up 7 days after all destinations have completed or been cancelled; media still needed by queued or uncertain requests is preserved. This does not delete platform-published content. Online data is needed while you use the service, subject to platform refresh/deletion requirements and your privacy requests.

GOPost provides a 30-day retention period after trial or subscription expiry so you can recover eligible workspace data by resuming access. Expiry does not by itself disconnect social accounts. This is a data-retention period, not free continued access to paid features. The lifecycle job removes stored drafts, posts, conversations, insights, AI outputs and usage/operation records at its next run after that deadline. A record of a post in GOPost is separate from the published post on the social platform. The job is not yet deployed, so automatic cleanup is not currently active.

Account profile, social connection metadata and credentials remain while you keep those connections and the GOPost account, so expiry alone does not disconnect them. They are removed through disconnection or account deletion as applicable. OAuth states and unconfirmed grants expire after 10 minutes; raw webhook events have a 7-day retention deadline. Expired temporary records are removed by a subsequent lifecycle run. A minimal deletion-completion record is kept for 30 days to prevent stale requests recreating deleted data, then removed. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and any applicable legal obligations. No separate GOPost media-upload storage or backup system is currently active. Platform-specific retention and revocation requirements take precedence over the general recovery period.

To enforce the one-trial-per-social-account rule, a keyed hash of the social identity and the original trial dates are retained while that rule remains part of the service. Account deletion removes the deleted GOPost user identifier from these claims. The hash remains pseudonymous and is used only for trial-abuse prevention, not advertising; contact us to exercise any applicable rights concerning it.

We may need to keep limited records to satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse. Where retained, they should be limited to those purposes; deleting GOPost data does not delete copies retained independently by a social platform or copies you exported.

8. Security and international processing

The Android app encrypts its local draft cache using Android Keystore-backed keys and disables its configured cloud backup. Backend authorization is tied to the signed-in user's workspace. Social authorization tokens are encrypted separately on the server when those integrations are configured; AI API keys are kept server-side and are not included in the Android app.

Google, Meta, Z.ai and other service providers may process data outside your country. Z.ai's API addendum describes processing generally from Singapore. Applicable transfer safeguards and service-region choices must be assessed before public release. No system or encryption method guarantees complete security, particularly on a compromised device.

9. Children and sensitive information

Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in drafts, AI requests or support messages. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us for review and appropriate removal.
