Law365 · Task Reminders

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Android application ID: com.gopost.app

Your tasks stay on your phone. Task Reminders works offline. The app does not send your tasks to Law365, and it has no account login, advertising or analytics service.

1. About this policy

Law365 publishes Task Reminders, an Android app for creating tasks, setting their dates and times, and keeping track of pending and completed tasks. This policy explains how the current app handles information and how to contact us about privacy.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information needed to manage your tasks:

  • The task names you enter.
  • The dates and times you choose.
  • Whether each task is pending or completed.
  • A randomly generated identifier for each task, used locally to distinguish tasks. This is not a device or advertising identifier.

This information is saved in the app’s private storage on your Android device. It is used to display, edit, sort, complete and delete your tasks. Law365 does not receive or remotely access this task information through the app.

3. Collection, sharing and tracking

The app does not transmit task content or other user data to our servers. It does not require an account, connect to social accounts, display ads, or include a third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

We do not sell, rent or share your locally stored task information. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier. It does not request internet access, notification permission or exact-alarm access.

Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may separately process information under their own settings and privacy policies. That platform processing is separate from the app’s local task storage.

4. Retention and deletion

Your tasks remain in the app’s local storage until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Marking a task completed does not delete it.

  • Delete an individual task: open its options, choose Delete and confirm.
  • Remove all local app data: use Android Settings → Apps → Task Reminders → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names may vary by device.
  • Uninstall: Android normally removes the app’s private data when you uninstall it.

The app does not offer cloud sync or an online task backup. Its backup configuration disables Android cloud backup and excludes its saved task file from configured device transfer. Device-level behavior can vary; we cannot restore deleted local tasks from a Law365 server because we do not hold a server copy.

There is no app account to delete. You do not need to email us to remove tasks stored on your phone.

5. Security

The app uses Android’s app-private storage to restrict ordinary access by other apps. It does not add a separate password or app-level encryption to the task file. Keep your device protected with a screen lock and current security updates. No storage method can guarantee complete protection, particularly on an unlocked, rooted or compromised device.

6. Information you send to support

If you choose to email gopost72@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Please do not send passwords, signing keys or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, Gmail. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to those obligations. Your local task list is not automatically attached to an email.

7. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children through its offline task features, just as it does not collect that information from other users. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us so we can review the request and remove the information where appropriate.

8. This privacy-policy webpage

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or external fonts. The provider hosting the page may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, browser information and access logs, to deliver and secure the webpage under its own policies. Visiting this page does not give it access to tasks saved in the Android app.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing a new use of personal information. This policy describes the current Task Reminders app.
